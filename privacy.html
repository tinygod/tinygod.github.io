棱光叠塔 Prism Stack 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日

中文

《棱光叠塔》（下称“本游戏”）是一款免费游戏，通过 Google AdMob 展示广告获得收入。本政策说明本游戏及其中的广告服务如何处理数据。

1. 我们自己不收集的数据

本游戏没有账号系统，开发者不运营服务器，不收集你的姓名、邮箱、联系人等信息。你的最高分只保存在本机，不会上传；删除游戏后即被清除。

2. 广告 SDK（Google AdMob）收集的数据

本游戏集成了 Google Mobile Ads SDK（AdMob）和 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP，用于管理广告同意）。根据这两个 SDK 的官方隐私清单，可能收集以下数据：

  • 设备标识符：广告标识符（IDFA，仅在你允许追踪时）、或其他限定于本 App/开发者的设备标识符。与你的设备关联，并且是唯一用于追踪（跨 App 和网站投放广告）的数据。
  • 粗略位置：根据 IP 地址推断的大致位置（不使用 GPS 精确定位）。与你关联，但不用于追踪。
  • 产品交互：App 启动、点击、视频观看等互动信息。与你关联，但不用于追踪。
  • 广告数据：你看过的广告等信息。与你关联，但不用于追踪。
  • 诊断数据：性能数据（启动时间、卡顿率、耗电等）、崩溃日志和其他诊断数据。不与你的身份关联，也不用于追踪。

用途：AdMob 将上述数据用于第三方广告（展示广告，在你同意时为个性化广告，否则为非个性化广告）、开发者广告或营销（如衡量和推广本游戏）以及分析（衡量广告效果、防欺诈、诊断和改进 SDK）；崩溃日志仅用于分析。UMP 为管理你的同意，会处理粗略位置（判断你所在地区是否需要显示同意弹窗）、产品交互（记录你在弹窗中的选择）和性能数据，这些仅用于 App 功能，不与你关联，也不用于追踪。

共享：以上数据由 Google 直接收集和处理，并可能按照 Google 的政策与参与展示广告的合作方共享。详见 Google 隐私权政策，以及 Google 如何使用使用其服务的网站或应用中的信息。我们不出售你的个人信息给任何人。

3. 你的选择与退出方式

  • App 跟踪透明度（ATT）：首次启动时系统会询问是否允许追踪。选择“要求 App 不跟踪”后，SDK 无法获取 IDFA。你随时可以在 iPhone「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」中关闭。
  • 广告同意设置（UMP）：在适用地区（如欧洲经济区、英国、瑞士及美国部分州），启动时会显示 Google 的同意弹窗。你可随时在游戏内「设置 → 隐私选项」中重新查看并修改选择。
  • 拒绝后你仍可正常游戏，只是看到的广告可能相关性较低。

4. 儿童隐私

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童（在欧洲经济区等地区为当地规定的数字同意年龄以下的儿童），也不在 App Store「儿童」类别中发布。我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。广告内容级别已限制为适合大众的级别。如果你是家长并认为孩子的数据被收集，请联系我们，我们会协助处理。

5. 欧洲用户（GDPR / UK GDPR）

  • 法律依据：个性化广告及在设备上存储或读取信息，基于你通过 UMP 弹窗给出的同意；非个性化广告、防欺诈和必要的诊断，基于合法利益或 Google 声明的其他依据。
  • 同意：你可随时在「隐私选项」中撤回同意，撤回不影响此前处理的合法性。
  • 你的权利：访问、更正、删除、限制处理、反对处理、数据可携带，以及向所在国数据保护机构投诉。由于开发者本身不持有你的数据，针对广告数据的请求可通过 Google 提供的工具行使，我们也会协助转达。

6. 美国加州及其他州用户（CCPA / CPRA）

我们不以金钱为对价“出售”个人信息。但为跨情境行为广告向 Google 提供设备标识符等信息，可能构成法律意义上的“共享”。你可以通过关闭 ATT 追踪或在「隐私选项」中选择退出来行使“请勿出售或共享我的个人信息”的权利。你还享有知情、删除、更正和不受歧视的权利。我们不收集敏感个人信息。

7. 政策变更与联系方式

本政策更新时会修改本页的生效日期。如有问题，请联系：wang.yorick@gmail.com。

English

Prism Stack (the “Game”) is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. This policy explains how the Game and its ad service handle data.

1. What we do not collect

The Game has no accounts and the developer runs no servers. We do not collect your name, email, or contacts. Your high score is stored only on your device, is never uploaded, and is deleted when you delete the Game.

2. Data collected by the ad SDK (Google AdMob)

The Game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP, used to manage ad consent). According to the official privacy manifests of these SDKs, they may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) or other app- or developer-scoped device identifiers. Linked to your device, and the only data used for tracking (ads across other companies’ apps and websites).
  • Coarse location: approximate location inferred from your IP address (no precise GPS location). Linked to you, not used for tracking.
  • Product interaction: app launches, taps, video views, and other interactions. Linked to you, not used for tracking.
  • Advertising data: such as ads you have seen. Linked to you, not used for tracking.
  • Diagnostics: performance data (launch time, hang rate, energy use), crash logs, and other diagnostic data. Not linked to your identity and not used for tracking.

Purposes: AdMob uses this data for third-party advertising (serving ads, personalized with your consent, otherwise non-personalized), developer advertising or marketing (such as measuring and promoting the Game), and analytics (measuring ad performance, preventing fraud, diagnosing and improving the SDK); crash logs are used only for analytics. To manage your consent, UMP processes coarse location (to tell whether your region requires the consent form), product interaction (to record your choices in the form), and performance data, for app functionality only; this data is not linked to you and not used for tracking.

Sharing: this data is collected and processed directly by Google and may be shared with partners involved in serving ads under Google’s policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Your choices and how to opt out

  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): on first launch iOS asks whether to allow tracking. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the SDK cannot access the IDFA. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent (UMP): in applicable regions (such as the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and certain US states) Google’s consent form appears on launch. You can review or change your choices anytime in the Game under Settings → Privacy Options.
  • You can keep playing if you decline; ads may simply be less relevant.

4. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the digital age of consent in your country, e.g. in the EEA) and is not listed in the App Store Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content is limited to general-audience ratings. If you are a parent and believe your child’s data was collected, contact us and we will help.

5. Users in Europe (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Legal basis: personalized ads and storing or accessing information on your device rely on your consent given through the UMP form; non-personalized ads, fraud prevention, and necessary diagnostics rely on legitimate interests or other bases stated by Google.
  • Consent: you may withdraw consent anytime in Privacy Options; this does not affect processing before withdrawal.
  • Your rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Because the developer holds none of your data, requests about ad data can be made through Google’s tools; we will also help forward them.

6. California and other US state residents (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell personal information for money. However, providing device identifiers and similar data to Google for cross-context behavioral advertising may count as “sharing” under the law. You can exercise your right to opt out of sale or sharing by turning off ATT tracking or opting out in Privacy Options. You also have the rights to know, delete, correct, and not be discriminated against. We do not collect sensitive personal information.

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Questions: wang.yorick@gmail.com.
