中文

怎么玩？光尘从倾倒口不停落下，管道固定不变。点按闸门：分流闸在两个出口之间切换，限流闸在畅行和关闭之间切换。把光尘送进尘泻口保持畅通，吞吐够了棱晶就会落下；等「可护送」亮起，再把闸门拨向棱晶出口。棱晶进入棱晶出口就算送达。

想再看一遍教学？在游戏内「设置 → 重看教学」。

为什么输了？失败时画面会停住，标出出事的格子、管段和最后一道闸门当时的朝向，结算卡上会写一句原因。常见的三种：入口压力满了（入口溢流）、棱晶流进了尘泻口（被泻走）、管道堵死或棱晶卡住（这里堵住了）。

后面的盘怎么解锁？通关上一盘即可解锁下一盘。

我的进度和最佳用时去哪了？进度、通关次数和最佳用时只保存在本机。删除重装游戏或更换设备后，记录不会保留。

需要联网或登录吗？不需要账号，离线也能玩。本游戏免费，含 Google AdMob 广告，联网时才会加载。对局中不显示广告。

怎么切换语言、关掉声音或震动？在游戏内「设置」里可以选语言（跟随系统 / 中文 / English），也可以分别开关音乐、音效和震动。

广告和隐私怎么设置？在欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士，首次启动会先显示广告同意表单；之后 iOS 会用系统弹窗询问是否允许追踪。在这三个地区，你可以随时在游戏内「设置 → 隐私选项」修改广告同意；任何地区都可以在 iPhone「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」关闭追踪。拒绝不影响游戏，只是广告相关性会降低。详见隐私政策。

遇到问题或有建议？可以在游戏内「设置 → 联系支持」直接写邮件，或发邮件至 wang.yorick@gmail.com，写明设备型号、iOS 版本、哪一盘，以及出了什么问题（截图更好）。

反馈时请附上什么？游戏版本号（「联系支持」会自动预填版本号和设备型号）、设备型号、iOS 版本、哪一盘、问题怎么复现；有截图或录屏更好。

隐私政策

English

How do I play? Dust keeps pouring from the inlet, and the pipes never change. Tap the gates: a Splitter switches between two outlets, and a Valve toggles between open and closed. Send the dust to the drains to keep things flowing. Once you've cleared enough, the prism drops in. When "Clear to escort" lights up, turn the gate toward the Prism Exit. The prism reaching the Prism Exit counts as a delivery.

Want to see the tutorial again? In the game, go to Settings → Replay tutorial.

Why did I lose? When you lose, the board freezes and marks the cell, the pipe, and the last gate along with which way it was set. The result card explains what happened in one line. The three most common causes are inlet overflow (the inlet pressure filled up), a drained prism (the prism flowed into a drain), and a jam (a pipe was blocked or the prism got stuck).

How do I unlock more boards? Clear a board to unlock the next one.

Where are my progress and best times? Your progress, clear counts, and best times are stored only on this device. They are not kept if you delete the game or switch devices.

Do I need an account or internet? No account is needed, and you can play offline. The game is free and shows Google AdMob ads, which load only when you're online. There are no ads during gameplay.

How do I change the language or turn off sound or haptics? In the game's Settings, you can choose the language (System, 中文, or English) and turn music, sound effects, and haptics on or off separately.

How do I manage ads and privacy? In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, you'll first see an ad consent form on first launch; after that, iOS asks with a system prompt whether to allow tracking. In those regions, you can change ad consent anytime in the game under Settings → Privacy options. Anywhere, you can turn off tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining doesn't affect gameplay. Ads may just be less relevant. See the Privacy Policy.

Problems or feedback? Use Settings → Contact support in the game, or email wang.yorick@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, which board you were on, and what went wrong. Screenshots help.

What should I include in feedback? The game version (Contact support fills in the version and device model for you), your device model, iOS version, which board, and how to reproduce the problem. Screenshots or a screen recording help.
