棱光叠塔 Prism Stack 技术支持

中文

怎么玩？方块在塔顶来回滑动，点击屏幕任意位置放下它。没对齐的部分会被切掉，完美对齐即为 Perfect，可连击。叠高之后，光一分为二，塔会分成两座。Perfect 会点亮光谱，折射也可能让这次落下弯曲。

我的最高分去哪了？最高分只保存在本机。删除重装游戏或更换设备后，记录不会保留。

需要联网或登录吗？不需要账号，离线也能玩。本游戏免费，含 Google AdMob 广告；联网时才会加载广告。

广告和隐私怎么设置？首次启动会先显示广告同意弹窗（适用地区），再由系统询问是否允许追踪。你可随时在游戏内「设置 → 隐私选项」修改广告同意，或在 iPhone「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」关闭追踪。拒绝不影响游戏，只是广告相关性降低。详见隐私政策。

遇到问题或有建议？请发邮件至 wang.yorick@gmail.com，并注明设备型号与 iOS 版本。

隐私政策

English

How do I play? Blocks slide across the top of the tower. Tap anywhere to drop one. Overhang is sliced off; a perfect alignment is a Perfect, and Perfects chain into combos. Later the tower splits into two. Perfects light the spectrum, and refraction can bend the drop.

Where is my high score? It is stored only on this device and is not kept if you delete the Game or switch devices.

Do I need an account or internet? No account is needed and you can play offline. The Game is free and shows Google AdMob ads, which load only when you are online.

How do I manage ads and privacy? On first launch you may see an ad consent form (in applicable regions), then iOS asks whether to allow tracking. You can change ad consent anytime in Settings → Privacy Options in the Game, or turn off tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining does not affect gameplay; ads may just be less relevant. See the Privacy Policy.

Problems or feedback? Email wang.yorick@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version.
